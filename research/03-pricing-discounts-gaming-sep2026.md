## Apple Certified Refurbished — THE value play of 2026
Sources: Gizmodo (Jul 20 2026), refurb.me (Aug 14 2026), RedFlagDeals (May 22 2026), Reddit.
- Apple refurb store: ~15% off; includes new battery+outer shell, full 1-yr warranty, AppleCare eligible. Refurb M5 MBP since Apr 2026; M5 Pro/Max since Jun 2026; M4 Air since ~Jun 2025.
- M4 Air 13" 16/256 refurb ≈ **$849–$859** (Back Market / refurb.me Aug 2026) vs $1,299 new M5 Air 16/512. Apple's own refurb M4 Air 16/256 was ~$849 US (Canada CAD 1,039).
- M4 Air was $999 new pre-M5 — so a refurb M4 Air is now cheaper than ANY new Apple laptop except the Neo, with 16GB. **Guide: this is the #1 value recommendation for budget-constrained CS students who want macOS.**
- Third-party: Back Market, Amazon Renewed, Best Buy Geek Squad Certified Refurb — check grade + warranty (Back Market 1yr).

## Gaming / dGPU laptops (RTX 50-series, 2025–2026)
Sources: PC Gamer best list (Jul 15 2026), Tom's Hardware LOQ Essentials 15 Gen 11 (Aug 4 2026), laptopmedia LOQ 15IPH11 (Jun 27 2026), PCMag deals (Aug 10 2026), Best Buy listings, Notebookcheck Zephyrus G14 2026 (Mar 4 2026).
- RTX 5050 (8GB), 5060 (8GB), 5070 (8GB), 5070 Ti (12GB), 5080 (16GB), 5090 (24GB) laptop GPUs. **VRAM matters for ML: 8GB is tight; 12GB+ (5070 Ti) is the useful floor for local training/inference; 16GB (5080) comfortable.**
- Budget: **Lenovo LOQ 15** (Ryzen 7 250 or Intel, RTX 5050/5060, 16GB DDR5 SO-DIMM upgradeable, 1TB, 144–165Hz IPS) ~$1,079 (PC Gamer "expect to pay"); seen $1,099 CAD sale w/ 5060. Tom's HW: "generous internal upgrade options." Plastic, mediocre battery. Exceldisc: "last affordable gaming laptop before the RAMpocalypse."
- Mid: **Lenovo Legion 5i Gen 10** ~$1,499 (CNET pick). Legion Pro 5/7. Asus TUF A14/A16. HP Omen.
- Premium 14": **Asus ROG Zephyrus G14 (2026)** — Ryzen 9 270 (verify: 2026 uses Ryzen AI 9 or Ryzen 9 270?) / RTX 5060–5070, 3K OLED 120Hz, 16–32GB. Best Buy $2,049 (5060/16GB). PCMag deal: 5060 config $1,599 (from $2,299); 15" OLED 5070 $1,899. Notebookcheck: "truly impressive... frame rigidity." 
- Premium 16": Razer Blade 16 ($2,400+; PC Gamer #1), Razer Blade 14, Lenovo Legion 9i, Asus ROG Zephyrus G16, MSI Stealth.
- Workstation: HP ZBook Power 16 G11 A (PCMag programmer pick), ThinkPad P14s Gen 7 (Panther Lake + RTX PRO 1000, 3.6 lb — StorageReview Aug 4 2026, leads CPU benchmarks), P1 Gen 8, Dell Pro Max.
- Strix Halo laptops (Ryzen AI Max+ 395, 8060S, up to 128GB unified): HP ZBook Ultra G1a 14", Asus ROG Flow Z13 (tablet). ~$2,000–4,000+. Good for local LLMs without dGPU; ROCm/Vulkan for llama.cpp.
- Guide stance on dGPU for CS students: NOT needed for 90%. Needed/nice for: game dev (Unity/Unreal), graphics courses, CUDA courses, local ML experimentation (cloud/Colab/university clusters are the realistic path for real training), and gaming on the side. Cost: battery life, weight, noise, price, RAM budget.
