# Research: Apple laptop lineup as of Sep 2026

Sources: MacRumors MacBook Pro roundup (updated ~Sep 4 2026), PCMag M5 MacBook Air 13 review (Jun 25 2026, w/ editor's note on price hike), Mashable, RTINGS (Aug 28 2026), AppleInsider (Mar 30 2026).

## CRITICAL CONTEXT: June 25, 2026 Apple price hike
Apple raised prices across MacBooks citing memory/storage cost increases (global DRAM shortage):
- MacBook Air: +$200. 13" now **$1,299** base (was $1,099 at March 2026 launch; M4 was $999). 15" now **$1,499** (was $1,299).
- MacBook Pro: +$300. 14" M5 now **$1,999** base; 14" M5 Pro **$2,499**; 16" M5 Pro **$2,999**.
- MacBook Neo also affected (PCMag headline: "Buying MacBook Neo, iPad? Get ready to pay more").

## MacBook Air (M5, released March 2026)
- Chip: M5, 10-core CPU (4 "super" cores + 6 efficiency cores), 8-core GPU (base) or 10-core GPU. 16-core Neural Engine. Each GPU core has Neural Accelerator.
- Memory: 16GB base, configurable 24GB / 32GB. Unified memory bandwidth 153GB/s.
- Storage: **512GB base** (doubled from M4's 256GB), up to 4TB. SSDs ~2x faster than M4 Air.
- Display: 13.6" 2560x1664 Liquid Retina (IPS LED), 60Hz, 500 nits, P3. 15.3" version 2880x1864. NO OLED, NO ProMotion. Notch.
- Ports: 2x Thunderbolt 4 (USB-C) on left + MagSafe 3; 3.5mm jack on right. Supports 2 external displays (6K60) with lid open.
- Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 (Apple N1 chip).
- 12MP Center Stage webcam. Quad speakers. Touch ID.
- Fanless. 0.44" thick, 2.7 lb (13"). 15" ~3.3 lb.
- Battery: PCMag video rundown ~18 hours (shorter than M4 Air's result, but still excellent). Apple claims 18h.
- 40W dynamic (60W max) adapter included in US.
- Preconfigs (pre-hike): $1,099 (8-GPU, 16/512), $1,299 (10-GPU, 16/1TB), $1,499 (10-GPU, 24/1TB). Post-hike add $200 to each → $1,299 / $1,499 / $1,699.
- Colors: Silver, Sky Blue, Starlight, Midnight.
- Verdict from reviewers: PCMag Editors' Choice; AppleInsider "incremental update but best buy for most"; Wirecutter top pick overall laptop.
- Benchmarks: Metal ~64,000 (M5 10-GPU) vs M4 ~55,000. M5 vs M4 CPU ~+15% MT; Apple says up to +20% in code compile.

## MacBook Pro 14 (M5, Oct 2025) and 14/16 (M5 Pro/Max, March 2026)
- Design unchanged since 2021. 14.2" 3024x1964 / 16.2" 3456x2234, 254ppi, mini-LED Liquid Retina XDR, 1000 nits SDR, 1600 peak HDR, ProMotion 120Hz. Nano-texture +$150.
- M5 (base 14" only): 10-core CPU/10-core GPU, 16GB base (up to 32GB), 153GB/s. **1TB SSD now base.** 3x Thunderbolt 4. Wi-Fi 6E/BT 5.3 (older). 72.4Wh. 24h video/16h web. Fan-cooled. 3.4 lb.
- M5 Pro: 15-core CPU/16-GPU or 18-core CPU/20-GPU. 24GB base, up to 64GB. 307GB/s. 3x Thunderbolt 5. Wi-Fi 7/BT6. "Fusion Architecture" dual-die. Up to 6 super cores + 12 performance(efficiency) cores.
- M5 Max: 18-core CPU, 32- or 40-core GPU. 36GB base, up to 128GB. 460/614GB/s. 2TB base. 
- All: HDMI 2.1, SDXC, MagSafe 3, headphone jack w/ high-impedance support, 6-speaker system, 12MP camera. 
- 16": 100Wh battery, 140W adapter, 4.7 lb.
- High Power Mode on Pro/Max.
- Geekbench 6: M5 ~4,263 SC / 17,862 MC. M5 Max ~4,268 SC / 29,233 MC (faster than M3 Ultra).
- Prices (post-June-2026 hike):
  - 14" M5 16/1TB $1,999; 24/1TB $2,199; 32/1TB $2,399. (There WAS a 16/512 config at $1,599 shown as N/A — discontinued/legacy.)
  - 14" M5 Pro 15c 24/1TB $2,499; 18c 24/2TB $3,199; M5 Max 36/2TB $4,099.
  - 16" M5 Pro 24/1TB $2,999; 48/1TB $3,599; M5 Max 32GPU 36/2TB $4,399; 40GPU 48/2TB $4,999.
- Refurb: Apple refurb store has M5 MBP (since Apr 2026) and M5 Pro/Max (since Jun 2026).

## What's next (rumors — flag as rumors in guide)
- Base 14" MBP → **M6 (2nm)** expected **fall 2026** (MacRumors says "not a good time to buy" base 14" MBP).
- OLED + touchscreen "MacBook Ultra" late 2026/early 2027, with M5 Pro/Max (no M6 Pro/Max; skipping to M7 Pro/Max in 2027). Thinner/lighter redesign.
- Air: unchanged design since M2 (2022); reviewers repeatedly ask for OLED/120Hz.

## Implications for the guide
- The "M5 Air 16/512" at $1,299 is still the default recommendation for most CS students who want a Mac — but the value proposition has been hit by +$200.
- MacBook Neo could be the budget Mac story IF RAM is workable — verify.
- Base MBP 14 M5: wait for M6 if buying in Sep–Nov 2026.
- Refurb M4 Air/Pro is now a very strong value play given new-price inflation.
- Education pricing: Apple typically $100 off Air, $200 off Pro + back-to-school promo (verify 2026 promo).
